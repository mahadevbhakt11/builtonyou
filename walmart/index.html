A taste of page 11

Should you cut your price? Here's the maths.

Sam Walton's famous rule: buy for 80 cents, sell for a dollar instead of $1.20, and sell so much more that you make more money. It's true, but only if volume rises enough. Most businesses never check.

The break-even volume formula
Volume needed = Old unit profit ÷ New unit profit

$0.40 → $0.20 profit per item = you need 2× the sales just to stand still.

The playbook walks you through when the cut pays, when it kills you, and gives you a worksheet to run it on your own products.
